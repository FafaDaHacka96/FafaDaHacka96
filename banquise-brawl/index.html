Making a throw

  1. When you aren't spinning, your flipper hangs toward the snow. Hold grab to scoop a snowball.
  2. Keep holding grab and stand still to roll it bigger. The snow under you sinks, then slowly grows back.
  3. Hold spin to wind up. The flipper keeps accelerating, but heavy snowballs spin slower.
  4. Let go of grab to throw. The ball leaves along the flipper's path: behind your head gives a lob, straight up gives a line drive. Dots show the start of the arc.
  • Big, fast snowballs hit harder and shove harder.
  • Moving, jumping and spinning use stamina. Below 70% you slow down. Bare ice is slippery.
  • Your ultimate charges over time, and faster when you hit or get hit. In the last 60 seconds a blizzard makes it charge 2.5× faster.
  • When time runs out, the most HP wins. A tie goes to sudden death.
